Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy explains the information BoundaryLive uses when you visit the website. Please do not submit passwords, payment details or other sensitive personal information through the website.

Browser preferences

BoundaryLive stores your selected language in your browser's local storage so the preference can be remembered on later visits.

Polls

If you vote in a poll, the selected option and a randomly generated browser token are sent to our database to record the vote and help prevent repeat voting in the same poll. The token is not your account login. You can clear browser storage, but doing so may affect the website's ability to recognise a previous vote from that browser.

Contact form

If you send a message, we store the subject and message, along with any name or email address you choose to provide. Name and email are optional. Messages are available to authorised BoundaryLive administrators for handling enquiries and feedback.

YouTube embeds

Live video is embedded using YouTube's privacy-enhanced embed domain. When you play or interact with an embedded video, YouTube may collect information and use cookies or similar technologies under its own privacy policy and terms.

Advertising and third parties

BoundaryLive may display advertisements supplied by third-party advertising partners. Those partners may use cookies, device identifiers or similar technologies to deliver, measure or personalise advertising, subject to their own policies and applicable settings. Third-party sites and services have privacy practices that BoundaryLive does not control. Please review the relevant provider policies for details.

Data requests

You may use the contact form to ask about a message you submitted or request its deletion. Please provide enough information to identify the submission without sending sensitive credentials.

Changes

This policy may be updated when website features or third-party services change. The latest revision date appears at the top of this page.
