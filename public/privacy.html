Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Information we use

BoundaryLive stores your language preference and a random browser-generated poll token in your browser storage. If you vote in a poll, the poll option and token are stored in our database to help prevent repeat submissions for the same poll. We do not ask viewers to create an account to watch or vote.

Contact form

If you send us a message, we store the message and its subject, along with any name or email address you choose to provide. Name and email are optional. Contact messages are visible only to authorised BoundaryLive administrators and are used to respond to enquiries and manage feedback. Please do not include passwords, payment details or other sensitive information.

Embedded video

Live video is embedded from YouTube using its privacy-enhanced embed domain. When you play or interact with the player, YouTube may collect information under its own privacy policy and terms.

Advertising

Ad spaces may appear around the video player. No advertising partner is identified as active by this policy. If third-party advertising is enabled, this policy should be updated with the provider and relevant data practices before ads are activated.

Data requests

You may use the contact form to ask about a message you submitted or request its deletion. Include enough information for us to identify the submission, but do not send sensitive account credentials.
