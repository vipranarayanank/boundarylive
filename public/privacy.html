Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Information we use

BoundaryLive stores your language preference and a random browser-generated poll token in your browser storage. If you vote in a poll, the poll option and token are stored in our database to prevent repeat submissions for the same poll. We do not ask viewers to create an account.

Embedded video

Live video is embedded from YouTube. When you play or interact with the embedded player, YouTube may collect information under its own privacy policy and terms.

Advertising

Advertising may be displayed around the video player. If advertising partners are enabled, they may use cookies or similar technologies as described in their own privacy policies. This notice will be updated with partner details before ads are activated.

Contact details will be published here before public launch.
