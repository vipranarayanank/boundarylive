About BoundaryLive

BoundaryLive is an independent cricket match companion designed to help fans find the featured match, view match information and access an embedded live stream in one simple place.

What you can find here

Depending on the match currently configured, the website may show the match title, tournament, scheduled date and time, venue, teams, playing XI, toss information, points table and a match poll. The Live Match page provides the stream when one is available.

How live video works

Video is embedded from YouTube. BoundaryLive does not host or store the video stream. Availability, playback, live chat and any restrictions are controlled by YouTube and the channel providing the stream.

Match information

Match details are maintained for the featured match and may change as information becomes available. Please check official tournament or team sources for time-sensitive decisions, final playing XIs and official results.

Independence

BoundaryLive is an independent website and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the ICC, BCCI, any cricket board, team, tournament organiser or YouTube unless expressly stated.
